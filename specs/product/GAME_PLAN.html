  • Content to make
    • 5 rooms, each with 2 dirt levels and about 8 objects.
    • Cloth, sponge, broom, duster as tools.
    • Voice for each room: a short "what a mess" and a "so clean" line.
  • ProgressionEach clean room is a star. Five stars open the next house.
  • Risks
    • Only wave-1 templates are needed, so this is the cheapest game. The risk is repetition: vary the gesture per room.
  • First playable sliceLiving room: erase the dust and put the toys away. Done when a child cleans the room without help and asks to play again. This is also the first slice of the whole line.First game built. It starts the new repository and the core boundary inside it (addons/quiqui_core). It needs no new template, so it proves the player and the wave-1 templates with the least risk.
