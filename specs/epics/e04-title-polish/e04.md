# e04: Title and polish (backlog slice S4)

Goal: the game opens on a title screen, a parent can reach a small parent area behind a gate a child cannot pass by
tapping, every room ends with a before-and-after rub reveal, and voice lines can be played by key and locale.
Scope in: `t0_title`, parent gate, parent area with "reset progress", `t34_rub_reveal`, voice playback by key and
locale, a check script that lists missing voice files.
Scope out (needs people, not code): the recorded voice files themselves, final art. The "before" pictures of the
reveal are generated placeholders and stay placeholders until S4 art arrives.

## Stories (WSJF order)

| Story | Title | Why here |
|---|---|---|
| e04s01 | Voice by key and locale | ADR-003; rooms already carry `intro_voice` |
| e04s02 | Parent gate | A child must not reach the parent area by tapping |
| e04s03 | Template t34_rub_reveal | Before and after, last step of each room |
| e04s04 | Template t0_title and parent area | Play button, lock, reset progress |
| e04s05 | Boot opens on the title | The game starts at the title, play leads to the map |

## Decisions

- Parent gate: press and hold the lock for 2.5 seconds. A tap or a short press does nothing and shows only a ring that
  fills. Releasing early resets it. It needs no reading, which is the charter. Holding is not a timer that ends a step.
- The parent area has words (ADR-003 allows text there): an offline note, "Reset progress" and "Close". Reset clears
  clean rooms and coins and saves. No other setting exists.
- Voice: a missing file plays nothing and logs a warning. ADR-003 says a missing locale file is a gate failure, but no
  recordings exist yet, so `scripts/check_voice.sh` reports missing keys as a NOTE and has a `--strict` mode for the day
  the recordings arrive.
- `SceneDef.show_outro` (default true): the title leaves straight to the map without a second play button.
- Placeholder art again for the reveal "before" pictures (generated by script from the room backgrounds).

## Acceptance (epic)

- The game opens on the title; the play button leads to the map.
- A short press on the lock does nothing; a 2.5 s hold opens the parent area; reset clears progress.
- Each room ends with a rub reveal and the full house flow still completes.
- Preflight green, `bash scripts/trace_stories.sh --strict` passes, only two `.tscn` files exist.
