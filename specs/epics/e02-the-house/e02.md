# e02: The house (backlog slice S2)

Goal: the child picks a room on a house map, cleans it, sees the room turn clean on the map, and does it for five rooms.
Scope in: stage and layout on 4:3 and 16:9, `t21_touch_each`, two dirt levels, house state, room meter, `t25_map`,
navigation between map and rooms, four more rooms as data, `GeometryOracle`.
Scope out: reward screen, save, stars and coins (S3), title and parent gate, recorded voice, final art (S4).

## Decisions

- Home leaves to the map. The outro button continues to the map. "Play again" is tapping the room on the map again,
  which resets that room (S1 asked for play again; the map is now the way).
- Placeholder art again: dust, cloud and sparkle badges. Final art stays in S4.

## Acceptance (epic)

- From the map a child opens each of the five rooms, finishes it, and the map shows it clean.
- Every hit target is at least 120x120 and inside the screen at 16:9, 4:3 and iPad sizes.
- Preflight green, `bash scripts/trace_stories.sh --strict` passes, only two `.tscn` files exist.
